The 75 Hard Challenge is a mental toughness program created by Andy Frisella, not just a fitness challenge. It has *no modifications*, and if you miss *any* rule, you must start over from Day 1.

Here are the rules:

1.  **Follow a Diet:** Choose a strict diet plan and follow it precisely. There are *no cheat meals* and *no alcohol* for the entire 75 days.
2.  **Two 45-Minute Workouts:** Complete two separate 45-minute workouts every day.
    *   One of these workouts *must* be outdoors, regardless of weather conditions.
    *   The two workouts cannot be back-to-back; there must be a significant break between them.
3.  **Drink 1 Gallon of Water:** Consume one gallon (approximately 3.78 liters) of plain water daily.
4.  **Read 10 Pages of a Non-Fiction Book:** Read 10 pages from a non-fiction book that is aimed at personal development or learning.
    *   Audiobooks do *not* count.
5.  **Take a Daily Progress Picture:** Take a full-body picture of yourself every single day.
6.  **No Missed Steps (Restart):** If you miss *any* of the above rules, even one, for even one day, you *must* start over from Day 1 of the challenge. There are no "make-up" days or excuses.

The challenge is designed to build discipline, mental fortitude, and healthy habits over 75 consecutive days. It's crucial to understand that its creator emphasizes the strict adherence to *all* rules without compromise.

**Important Note:** Before starting any intensive program like 75 Hard, it's highly recommended to consult with a healthcare professional or doctor, especially if you have any pre-existing health conditions.
